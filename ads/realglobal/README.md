## Step 1: today, in the Google Ads app (15 minutes)

1. **Paste the negative keywords.**
   - Go to Campaigns → Real 4 → Negative keywords → **+**.
   - Paste every line of `1-negativas-pegar-en-app.txt` and save.
   - This blocks the "show me the Bible text" searches: Reina Valera, online, PDF, verses, specific translations, and so on.
2. **Pause or tighten the keyword that drags CTR down.**
   - Go to Search keywords and set the date to *Last 30 days*.
   - Scroll the table sideways to find CTR.
   - **Pause every keyword with CTR under 3% and more than 100 impressions.** "leer la biblia completa" (phrase) is almost certainly one of them.
   - Re-add it as **exact match** `[leer la biblia completa]` in the same ad group.
3. **Locations.**
   - Go to Real 4 → Settings → Locations.
   - Make sure the countries you actually serve are listed: Colombia, Mexico, United States, and the rest.
   - Choose **Presence: people in or regularly in your targeted locations**. The app may not offer this choice; if it doesn't, do it in the web version.
   - If Argentina is not a priority, remove it. Otherwise lower its share by removing it for a week while CTR recovers.
4. **Turn off auto-apply recommendations.**
   - In the web version: Recommendations → Auto-apply → untick everything.
   - Your 88.6% optimization score suggests Google may be applying changes for you.
