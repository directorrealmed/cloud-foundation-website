Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Cloud Foundation Inc. ("we," "us," or "our") operates the cloudfoundation.us website (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways:

  • Contact Form: When you submit our contact form, we collect your name, email address, phone number (optional), and message content.
  • Donation Information: When you make a donation, payment information is processed securely by third-party payment processors (Zelle, credit card processors, etc.). We do not store payment card details.
  • Automatic Information: When you visit our Site, we may automatically collect certain information about your device and browsing activity through cookies and similar technologies.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and requests
  • Process donations and send donation receipts
  • Send communications about our programs and impact
  • Improve and optimize our website
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information. Our website uses HTTPS encryption to secure data transmission. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website uses third-party services including:

  • Netlify: Web hosting platform (see Netlify Privacy Policy)
  • Google Analytics: Website analytics (optional, may be disabled)
  • Payment Processors: Zelle and credit card processors for donations

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt-out of communications

To exercise these rights, contact us at director@cloudfoundation.us.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Site is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date and posting the new policy on this page.
