What a Transformation Center is

Communities under pressure are rarely short of one thing. They are short of several at once — food, schooling, work, and someone to lead. A Transformation Center brings a feeding point, an educational programme, startup training and a leadership hub into a single building, so a family does not have to travel to four different agencies to be helped once.

It is deliberately unglamorous architecture. Cane walls, a metal roof, a concrete slab, second-hand desks. What makes it work is not the building but the fact that it is permanent, in the community, and open on a schedule people can rely on.

What the $20,000 covers

  • The structure

    Timber frame, cane walls, corrugated metal roof, and a poured concrete slab so the floor does not turn to mud in the rains.

  • The furniture

    Desks and chairs, bought second-hand wherever second-hand will do, which is almost everywhere.

  • The education supplies

    Workbooks, materials and the everyday consumables that turn an empty room into a school. This is the part most build budgets quietly leave out.

  • The labour

    Raised largely by the community that will use it, working alongside our team.

Villa Luz, a barrio in Cali, is our urban centre. La Guajira is the rural one.
