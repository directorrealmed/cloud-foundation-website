Questions.

Is my gift tax-deductible?

Cloud Foundation Inc. is a tax-exempt organisation under section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, EIN 88-2337360. Gifts from United States taxpayers are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law, and you will receive an acknowledgement for your records.

Can I give to a specific programme?

Yes. A gift to a named campaign is used for that campaign. After you give, write to director@cloudfoundation.us and tell us which programme. If you would rather we used it wherever the need is greatest, say “unrestricted” and we will.

How does the money get to Colombia?

Cloud Foundation Inc. receives the gift in the United States and funds the programme through Fundación Cloud, our Colombian entity, constituted in 2018, which employs and directs the staff and volunteers who deliver the work.

Can I give by Zelle or cheque instead?

Yes. Zelle: give@fundacioncloud.com (put the programme name in the memo). Cheque: Cloud Foundation Inc., 16969 NW 67th Avenue, Suite 208, Hialeah, FL 33015. For a major gift, talk to us.

Who do I contact if something looks wrong?

Write to director@cloudfoundation.us and say so plainly. Joel Manderfield reads that address.
