Flagship

A permanent ministry campus — a home for discipleship, leadership training and community programmes that does not depend on rented rooms and borrowed time.

$500,000

Down payment we are raising toward a property of approximately $3 million.

Where we cannot count something yet, we say so rather than estimating. That is a slower way to raise money and a better way to keep a donor.
