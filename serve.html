What serving actually looks like

If you are hoping for a week that feels transformative, this may disappoint you. If you are willing to peel potatoes, carry boxes and do the same thing again on Thursday, you will be genuinely useful and we would be glad to have you.

Anyone who will work with children, young people or survivors of trafficking is screened before they begin. There are no exceptions, including for short trips.

Tell us what you can do

We read everything that comes in and we will tell you honestly whether we need what you are offering. A short, specific message is more useful than a long enthusiastic one.

We aim to reply within one week.

If money is the more useful thing you have

$5 feeds one person in El Calvario. $2,250 runs the kitchen for a full week — all three serving days, 450 meals.
