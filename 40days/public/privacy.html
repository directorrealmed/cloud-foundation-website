Personal Data Privacy Policy

Iglesia Real's “40 Days” app (English edition) · Version 2026-10 · Effective October 22, 2026

In short: we use your data so you can follow the 40-day plan, to walk with you in your faith if you'd like us to and, only if you agree, to send you resources and invitations. We don't sell your data or share it with third parties for commercial purposes. You can view it, correct it or delete your account whenever you want.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Iglesia Real, Tax ID (NIT) 901.716.252, located at Calle 19A #27-250, Medellín, Colombia. Phone: +57 304 306 2867. Email for personal data matters: real@fundacioncloud.com.

This policy is issued under Colombia's Statutory Law 1581 of 2012, Decree 1377 of 2013 (compiled in Decree 1074 of 2015) and other Colombian personal data protection rules (habeas data).

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email and password. Your password is stored encrypted; nobody, not even administrators, can see it.
  • Contact and location data: WhatsApp number (if you give it), country and city.
  • Data about your faith journey: whether you attend a church, whether you ask for prayer and whether you decide to receive Jesus.
  • Age confirmation: that you are 18 or older, or have permission from a parent or guardian.
  • Usage data: the readings you mark as complete, your participation in the Zoom prayer and the social media fast, the Bible version you choose, your reading group and who invited you.
  • Prayer wall: the requests, thanks or testimonies you post, the times you tap “Pray” and any reports you make.
  • Reminders: if you turn them on, a technical identifier for your browser so we can send you notifications.
  • Record of your consent: the date you accepted this policy and whether you agreed to receive communications.

3. Sensitive data

Your participation in a faith community, your spiritual decisions, your church and what you write on the wall or in your requests (for example, about your health or family) may be sensitive data under article 5 of Law 1581 of 2012. That's why:

  • Answering these questions is optional. You don't have to give us sensitive data, and no essential service depends on it.
  • What you post on the wall is visible to other users of the app. You can post as “Anonymous” and delete your posts at any time.
  • Private prayer requests and your faith decisions are only seen by Iglesia Real's Connection and prayer team.
  • We recommend not including full names, phone numbers, addresses or other people's data without their permission.

4. How we use your data

  • To create and protect your account and save your progress in the 40-day plan.
  • To show you your reading group. Members of your group see your first name and your reading progress.
  • Pastoral care: if you don't have a church, ask for prayer or decide to receive Jesus, our Connection team may contact you by WhatsApp or email to pray with you and invite you to an online group or our live gatherings.
  • To send you reading reminders and emails that are part of the plan (welcome, daily reading and closing).
  • Only if you agree by checking the box: to send you resources, invitations and news from Iglesia Real by email and WhatsApp, including the next round of 40 Days. You can withdraw this permission at any time from Profile.
  • To produce aggregate statistics (for example, how many people are reading in each country). These don't identify anyone.

We don't use your data for third-party advertising, and we don't sell or transfer it to third parties for commercial purposes.

5. Who processes your data on our behalf

To run the service we use technology providers that process data only on our instructions: Netlify (hosting and data storage), Brevo (sending email) and your browser's notification services. Their servers may be outside Colombia, including in the United States and the European Union. By accepting this policy you authorize this international transfer and transmission, which is done with security measures such as password encryption and secure connections (HTTPS). Bible audio is provided by YouVersion; we don't send them your personal data.

6. Your rights

As the owner of your data, you have the right to:

  • Know, update and correct your personal data.
  • Ask for proof of the consent you gave us.
  • Be told how we have used your data.
  • Withdraw your consent and ask us to delete your data. You can do it yourself from Profile › Delete my account, or by writing to us.
  • File complaints with Colombia's Superintendence of Industry and Commerce (SIC) for violations of the law, after first going through the inquiry or complaint process with us.
  • Access your personal data free of charge.

7. How to make an inquiry or complaint

Write to real@fundacioncloud.com, call +57 304 306 2867 or visit Calle 19A #27-250, Medellín, with your name, the email you signed up with and your request.

  • Inquiries: we reply within 10 business days at most. If we need more time we'll let you know, and the reply won't take more than 5 additional business days.
  • Complaints (correction, update, deletion or non-compliance): we reply within 15 business days at most. If we need more time we'll let you know, and the reply won't take more than 8 additional business days.

8. Minors

If you are under 18, you may only use the app with permission from your parent or legal guardian, and you confirm this when you sign up. We respect the best interests of children and adolescents and their prevailing rights.

9. How long we keep your data

While you have an active account, or until you ask us to delete it. Deleting your account erases your profile, your progress and the requests you posted. If you join the waiting list for the next round, we keep your name and email until that round or until you ask us to delete them.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make important changes to this policy, we'll let you know in the app and ask for your consent again.
